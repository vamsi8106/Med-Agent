SYNTHETIC DEMO GUIDELINE -- NOT REAL CLINICAL GUIDANCE. For exercising the RAG pipeline only.

1. Overview
This synthetic document describes an invented approach to managing type 2 diabetes in adults. It exists so the guideline retriever has something to find in demos and tests.

2. Glycemic Monitoring
In this synthetic guideline, glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) is reviewed at every routine visit for adults with type 2 diabetes. When HbA1c is above the individual target, the care team reviews adherence, diet, and the current medication regimen before any change is made.

3. Treatment Intensification
When HbA1c remains above target despite metformin, this synthetic guideline suggests reviewing whether adding a second agent is appropriate, taking into account hypoglycemia risk, cost, and comorbid hypertension. Combining a sulfonylurea with metformin increases hypoglycemia risk, so glucose monitoring is emphasised.

4. Hypertension in Diabetes
Blood pressure is checked at each visit. Patients with type 2 diabetes and hypertension are reviewed for kidney function at least once a year.

5. Sulfonamide Allergy
Patients with a documented sulfonamide allergy are flagged before a new drug is started, and the prescriber confirms cross-reactivity risk for the chosen agent.
